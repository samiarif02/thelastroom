## Decisions

- **Chapter 3 stays `playable: false` in this PR. Flip after briefs 35 and 37 merge.** The app
  treats the `missions` list as "every playable mission": the tower makes each one a room and the
  mission player assumes beats. Without brief 35's voice player, the tower would offer four
  missions nothing can play (wave 6: "The voice player (35) must land before the content that
  turns Chapters 2–3 playable"). So the seven missions ship as `chapterThreeMissions`, validated
  and tested as if Chapter 3 were open. **The flip is two lines:** spread `chapterThreeMissions`
  into `missions` and set Chapter 3 `playable: true`. `chapter_three_test`'s "waits for the voice
  player" test is the one to invert then.
- **Numbering:** `validateContent` required missions numbered 1, 2, 3… with no gaps. Chapter 2
  (8–14) is brief 37's, on another branch, so here 15 follows 7. The rule is now "a mission's
  number is its place in the campaign, after every title the earlier chapters tease". The result
  is the same when every chapter is written.
- **Salary figures are written in words** ("thirty to thirty-four thousand"). The character
  sheet's digit figures are Jordan's CV; a salary range is research, and only Mission 18 quotes
  one. Mission 21's follow-up says "the bottom of our band", so it never contradicts what a player
  picked in Mission 18.
- Voice missions are `hinted: false, timed: false`: there are no hint cards, and the recorder's
  `maxSeconds` is the limit, not the 60-second soft timer.
