# Status: brief 44 (every mission plays, start to finish)

Worker: background QA agent, worktree `../thelastroom-w11`, branch `test/44-play-every-mission`
(from `main` 785246b). Updated 2026-11-02.

## Why

Chapters 2 and 3 were written and validated as content (#41, #42), opened (#47) and given a
voice player (#44), but nobody had played Missions 8–21 through the real UI to their debrief.
The owner: "complete all the missions yourself".

## Done

- **`apps/mobile/test/features/campaign/every_mission_test.dart`** boots every mission in
  `missions` by its route (`/tower/mission/N`, the rooms before it played) and plays it through
  the real UI to the debrief, then on:
  - **choice missions (1–7, 15, 18, 19):** the lines, the draft step where offered (skipped),
    every beat (the first line, or the strong build on built beats: 4, 6, 7, 15), every "why"
    step, the follow-up the engine picks and its first reply;
  - **voice missions (8–14, 16, 17, 20, 21):** every line spoken (fake TTS; the test checks
    each interviewer line was said, addressed to the player), the answer and the reply recorded
    on a fake microphone, then scored twice, in two tests: by a fake AI verdict (`score-voice`
    on a fake backend) and offline by the self-check (two points ticked).
  - **Asserted for each:** the dossier shows the engine's (or the AI's) total; a lesson, a clean
    run or the guess slip (choices) or the lesson slip (voice); one attempt stored, with its
    score (and points made and source, for voice); the debrief's "+N XP" is this attempt's;
    `chapter_completed` fires on 7, 14 and 21 only; the debrief's button leads to the tower with
    this room lit, the room done and the next one open (Mission 7: its epilogue, then the
    Chapter 1 report; Mission 21: its epilogue, then the tower with every room done and the last
    room's card saying so); and no frame throws (`takeException` on every pumped frame,
    overflows included).
  - **Also:** Mission 7 → epilogue → Chapter 1 report → "Start Chapter 2" opens Mission 8; two
    missions in one sitting each show their own XP; only Mission 7 leads to a report.
  - **2x text and reduced motion:** Missions 4, 11, 15 and 21 (one per chapter, both kinds in
    Chapter 3) played again the same way at 375 × 667.
- `MissionRig` (the harness) takes extra overrides (the voice fakes) and unlocks rooms before
  a voice mission with a voice attempt.
- 38 tests, about 20 s. Every mission passes, at 1x, and the samples at 2x/reduced motion.
- **Played in Chrome** (release web build, headless, fake microphone): onboarding, Missions
  1–7 (choices), the epilogue, the Chapter 1 report, "Start Chapter 2", Missions 8–14 (voice,
  self-check), Mission 15 (choices, a built answer), 16–20, Mission 21 (voice, the epilogue, the
  last room), then Missions 8, 15 and 21 replayed on the fixed build. Stills in
  `.pr-screenshots/44/`.
