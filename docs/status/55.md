## Decisions

- **Verdicts** (`packages/engine/lib/src/reason_verdict.dart`, `ReasonVerdict.of`): no why step
  (built beat, pre-wave-5 attempt, or a reason stored on a beat that never asked:
  `ReasonPick.asked`) → no verdict; a guessed line → `guessed` (0); the right principle →
  `sound` (+10, `reasonSoundXp`); a considered wrong principle → `close` (+5, `reasonCloseXp`).
  Same split as the feedback sheet's tones (sound / close / guess).
- **Numbers:** a room is worth 20–100 XP (`missionXp`). Choice rooms ask why 2–3 times, so
  reasons add 0–30: under a third of a perfect first clear. Close earns half (XP rewards
  practice; the score rewards being right); random reasons average 6.7 a step vs 10 for knowing.
- **Voice rooms:** +5 per listen-for point made (`voicePointXp`). Saying a point is explaining
  it in your own words; voice scoring is content-only, and XP reads the same input
  (`madePointIds`). 6–7 points → 30–35, level with a choice room's 20–30. Missed point: 0.
- **Derived, never stored:** `MissionScore.reasonVerdicts` / `pointsMade`, rescored from the
  stored picks like the score. Old rows (no reasons) earn exactly what they did. A reason an
  older build stored on a built beat keeps its score (past results unchanged) but earns 0
  (`attempt_score.dart` marks it `unasked()`).
- **Unlocks:** nothing reads XP. Skills read dimension scores, rooms/floors open by clearing
  the room before, the weekly ring counts days. So no threshold moves.
- **Analytics:** no event carries XP; nothing added (no `reason_xp` field needed).
