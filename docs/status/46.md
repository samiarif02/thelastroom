## Round 2 (the owner's realme playthrough of #56)

- **P0, the stuck "Why that example?"**: reproduced on the realme (room 3, evidence beat, a
  quick pick of "I pick things up quickly…": her question, your line, then nothing). Root cause:
  a guess is called out *before* the why step, in the feedback sheet; the why bubble was already
  built and typing under that sheet, finished during the reaction phase (where `_whyTyped`
  ignores it), and when the why phase began the same bubble (same key, same line) was already
  complete, so `onComplete` never fired again and the phase never reached `reasons`. Fix: the
  why line only appears once she asks it (`whyAsked` in `mission_player.dart`). Tests:
  `conversation_test` (fails without the fix), `why_never_sticks_test` (every choice room,
  every why beat, read and guessed: Ch1 rooms 1–7 and Ch3's choice rooms).
- **Floors**: tapping a floor (or a window on a locked floor) opens `FloorSheet` in a new
  `ui/daylight/daylight_sheet.dart`: name, open/locked, who you meet, what it practises, how you
  answer, the seven rooms (check / up next / lock), what opens it; open rooms go straight in.
  `floor_previewed` registered first.
- **Debrief**: Daylight ground and everything around the sheet (chips, trust pips, XP tag,
  enamel skill arches, trap-tint cost card, lesson, reasoning wells, then/now, first try, CTA,
