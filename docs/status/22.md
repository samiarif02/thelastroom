Worker status file. Session thelastroom-w2-c1. Last update 2026-10-31 (Sat).

## Decisions

- Provider: **Gemini** (owner's key), `models/gemini-3.8-flash:generateContent`, temperature 0,
  `responseMimeType: application/json` + `responseSchema`; key in the `x-goog-api-key` header
  (never in a URL). Flash, not `gemini-4-argon`: the app waits at most 8 s.
- The AI score is its own table `public.ai_scores` (owner-only RLS), written by the function with
  the player's JWT: `mission_attempts` is append-only and the AI verdict arrives after the attempt
  row may already be synced, so columns there couldn't be filled reliably.
- Rate limit: `public.ai_score_usage` + security-definer `public.claim_ai_score()` (30/day per user,
  the limit inside SQL so a client can't raise it).
- App: `Backend.scoreAnswer` (the only `supabase_flutter` importer stays `supabase_backend.dart`);
  the debrief waits ≤ 8 s with "The panel is deliberating…", then shows the AI's numbers in the
  dossier ("Scored by AI · Senior recruiter") with a basis slip, or the engine's ("Offline score").
