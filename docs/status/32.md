## Decisions

- **The interview date stays on the device** (shared_preferences), not on the synced profile:
  - only the local reminders read it;
  - it avoids a drift schema migration and a Supabase migration;
  - a personal date stays off the server.
- **XP and unlocks come from the stored attempts' scores.** Until brief 30 stores reasons and
  decision times, a guessed or misreasoned beat reads a little high (w2-c1 is fixing that).
