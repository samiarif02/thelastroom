## Decisions

- **One font weight per family.** Newsreader with its optical-size axis (first direction) was
  270 KB and pushed mobile LCP to 4.7 s (performance 84). Lantern counts every request that
  finishes before the observed LCP, so font bytes, the gzipped HTML (kept under ~14.6 KB, one
  congestion window) and still sizes all matter.
- Tried and dropped: `experimental.inlineCss` (doubled the HTML), `content-visibility: auto` on
  sections (accessibility fell to 96: axe can't check skipped content).
- The mission index is real structure (21 rooms over three chapters); no figure on the page is a
  claim about users.
