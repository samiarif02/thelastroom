## Decisions

- **All sounds synthesised** in `tools/audio/` (original work, CC0). kenney.nl returns 403 to
  scripted downloads, so a downloaded pack couldn't be reproduced by a script; synthesis is.
- **OGG = Vorbis via `oggenc`** (`brew install vorbis-tools`): Homebrew ffmpeg has only the
  experimental Vorbis encoder, and SoLoud decodes Ogg Opus through a buffer-stream fallback that
  is riskier for looping ambience. M4A = AAC via ffmpeg.
- **Android `lowLatency: false`** (media stream, `mediaMusic`): the low-latency MMAP path can't be
  captured by screen recorders, and the lead records Android with scrcpy.
- `AudioService.startAmbience(int chapter)` takes the 1-based number (as `GameSounds.ambience`
  does) rather than content's `Chapter`. `startLoop(Cue)` covers the Recall Room pad.
- Ducking: `questionLands` and `followUpChallenge` duck; `answerLocked`, `dossierSlides` and
  `result` lift it. `scoreStamped` plays the stamp plus a 5-tick fading count-up.
- Title / chapter card / cliffhanger / locked floor follow the **Ambience/Music** volume (they're
  music-like); everything else follows Effects.

## Needs from the lead

- **iOS ambient session (mix with the player's music):** SoLoud leaves `AVAudioSession` to the
  app. The default (`soloAmbient`) respects the silent switch (the acceptance criterion) but
  stops the player's music. Mixing needs either `audio_session` (one dependency) or two lines in
  `ios/Runner/AppDelegate.swift` (`setCategory(.ambient)`): neither is in this brief's files.
- **Android audio focus:** SoLoud requests none, so "ducks on focus loss" is approximated by the
  app lifecycle (hidden → fade out, shown → room returns). Real focus ducking (a notification
  sound over the game) needs `audio_session`. Recommend adding it in brief 10 or a follow-up.
