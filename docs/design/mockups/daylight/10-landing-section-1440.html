Nerves fade when the questions stop being new.

Each room is one real question. Missed something? It comes back in your Recall Room on day 1, 3 and 7, until it sticks.

Meet the panel

Four people at Northstar Labs, each listening for something different. They react to what you say, and they ask the follow up you were hoping to avoid.

  1. 1

    Build the answer. Pick a line for each beat, or say it out loud from floor 2.

  2. 2

    Say why it works. Choosing the principle is what makes it stick.

  3. 3

    Read the debrief. Four scores, the reason for each, one lesson.
