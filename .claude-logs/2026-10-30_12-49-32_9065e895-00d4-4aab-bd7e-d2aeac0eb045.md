PR #25 is open and CI is green: https://github.com/samiarif02/thelastroom/pull/25. It's not merged, and it hasn't been tried with your real Gemini key: every test uses a fake Gemini. The lead does that live check with you.

**What the player sees:**
- After the reply, "The panel is deliberating…" shows for at most 8 seconds.
- Then the dossier shows the AI recruiter's four scores and overall, labelled "Scored by AI · Senior recruiter", with one sentence explaining each number. There's no advice, only the score and why.
- If there's no server, no internet, no answer within 8 seconds, the day's limit is spent, or anything goes wrong, it shows the built-in score labelled "Offline score". The player never sees an error.
- The built-in engine still scores every attempt, so offline play, the "You fell for" traps, the Recall Room and progress tracking work as before.

**How it works:**
- **The scoring call:** a new server function sends the answer to Gemini as a senior recruiter, using `gemini-3.8-flash`. I chose it over Google's bigger reasoning model so the verdict fits the 8-second wait.
- **What's sent:** the lines the player picked, the reasons they gave, which picks were guessed, the follow-up and the reply. Never the player's name or anything they typed.
- **Your key:** it stays on the server, sent in a request header rather than the URL, and never logged. I never read your `.env`.
- **Limits:** only a signed-in player can call it, and each player gets 30 AI scores a day.
- **Storage:** each verdict is saved in its own database table, visible only to that player.

**Running it with your key:** there's no hosted server, so it runs on your Mac and the phone reaches it over Wi-Fi. The README now has a three-step "AI scoring locally" section for that. To make that possible, debug and profile Android builds allow plain HTTP on the local network; release builds are unchanged.

**Testing:** all local checks pass, including 15 tests for the server function, 14 new database tests and 11 new app tests. I rebased onto the latest `main` before pushing. ADR 0006 records the decision and FR-24 in the requirements is rewritten.

**Decisions I've asked the lead to confirm:**
- **Where the AI score is saved:** in its own table, not as extra columns on the saved attempts as the brief said. Attempts can't be edited after they're saved, and they usually sync before the AI's answer comes back, so those columns could never be filled in.
- **Android:** the plain-HTTP change is in an area another brief owned.
- **CI:** the automated checks don't run the server function's tests yet. I ran them locally with Docker; adding them to CI is the lead's call.
