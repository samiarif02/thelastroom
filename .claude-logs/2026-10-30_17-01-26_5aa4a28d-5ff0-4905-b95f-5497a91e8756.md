If the onboarding looked unchanged, the app may also have skipped it because it remembers you already finished it on this phone. A fresh install would show the new onboarding.

## The real problem: random clicking still gets a score

Right now a mission is "read, tap one of three options, get scored". Nothing stops you from guessing, and guessing costs nothing, so there's no reason to care. I'd make these changes, most important first:

1. **Make guessing visible and costly.**
   - The options only appear after the interviewer finishes speaking.
   - A pick within about 1.5 seconds gets a reaction in the story ("You didn't even think about that, did you?"), trust drops, and that answer earns no score.
   - Random clicking stops being a shortcut and becomes a mistake the story reacts to.
2. **"Why?" after every choice.**
   - Once you pick an answer, the interviewer asks why, and you choose the reason (for example "shows impact", "answers what she actually asked", "admits a weakness honestly").
   - A random pick with a wrong reason scores low; the right answer for the right reason scores high. This is where the real learning happens, and it's hard to guess your way through.
3. **Build the answer instead of picking it.** For "tell me about a time…" questions, you drag answer pieces into order (Situation → Task → Action → Result) and choose the strong phrasing over the weak one. This is much more hands-on than A/B/C, and it teaches the method itself.
4. **Consequences in the scene, not just a number.**
   - Strong answers make the interviewer lean in and warm up.
   - Weak ones make her check her watch, get colder, or ask a harder follow-up.
   - The next question changes with how you're doing, so you feel the interview going well or badly as it happens.
5. **Spot the mistake.** Sometimes you hear "another candidate's" answer and tap the exact phrase that's weak. It's quick, it's a game, and it trains your ear.
6. **A short "here's what a great answer sounds like" after each mission,** one card per beat, and those cards come back later in the Recall Room so the lesson sticks.

## The UI

The tower and onboarding need a real redesign, not just new colours:
- clear chapter cards with a progress bar;
- the current mission obviously highlighted, with a big "Continue" button;
- characters on screen;
- more motion between screens.

## What I'd do before 5 November

Not all of this fits in five days. My recommendation:

- **Now (small):** the tower order fix (chapters and missions top to bottom), plus the header overlap from the screenshot.
- **Next (biggest win):** items 1, 2 and 4 together: anti-guessing, the "why?" step, and live consequences. They reuse the existing missions and scoring, so they're realistic in a day or two.
- **Then:** the tower and onboarding redesign, with item 3 (build-the-answer) for one or two missions if time allows.

Items 2 and 4 need new content: a reason list and reactions for each beat. That means touching the mission content and scoring engine, not just the screens.
